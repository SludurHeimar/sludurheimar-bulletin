House rules

Draft - review the wording, then this note can go.

What this is

A place to post text - ideas, stories, venting, complaints, whatever - for people in Iceland. Every post is reviewed before it goes public, stays up for a week, and there are no replies or comments.

What's not allowed

  • Racism or hate speech directed at a person or group.
  • Doxxing - phone numbers, home addresses, or similar identifying details about someone. Naming someone in connection with something they did is fine; publishing ways to find or contact them isn't.
  • Promoting illegal activity.
  • Advertising products or services.
  • Spam.

General opinions - including ones people disagree with, like wanting less immigration - are allowed as long as they don't cross into the above.

Note: some content can be illegal under Icelandic law regardless of these rules (for example, public hate speech under the Icelandic Penal Code). We can remove a post and may be required to, even if it doesn't clearly break a rule above.

Moderation

Every post is reviewed by a moderator before it's published. We can reject or remove a post, or ban an account, at our discretion.

Accounts

One account per person. Don't share your login. We can suspend or delete an account that repeatedly breaks these rules.

No warranty

This site is provided as-is. We're not responsible for the content people post, beyond our own moderation efforts.
